How to Download Pinterest Videos on iPhone

Tutorial Updated: November 2026 4 min read iOS 15 and later

Saving a Pinterest video on iPhone takes two parts. Safari downloads the file into the Files app, and then you move it into Photos yourself. Apple keeps those two places separate, which is why people often think the download didn't work. Here's the whole flow, including where the video goes.

What you need

An iPhone or iPad, Safari, and the link to the pin. You don't need an app from the App Store or a Pinterest login. KlickPint is a website, so there's nothing to install and nothing asking for access to your photos.

1

Copy the link in the Pinterest app

Open the video pin and tap the Share icon (the arrow), then Copy link. You'll get a short pin.it link, which is fine.

2

Open KlickPint in Safari and paste

Go to klickpint.online and tap Paste. Safari shows a small Paste bubble under your finger. Tap it to confirm. KlickPint starts looking up the video right away.

3

Tap a quality

You'll see a preview and the qualities Pinterest has for that pin, usually 1080p down to 360p. Tap one. Safari may ask "Do you want to download…?" Tap Download. A progress bar fills in on the page, and a small download arrow appears in Safari's address bar.

4

Move it into Photos

Open the Files app and go to Downloads. Tap the video to open it, tap Share, and choose Save Video. It now shows up in Photos with the rest of your camera roll.

Where did my video go?

By default Safari saves downloads to iCloud Drive → Downloads. If iCloud Drive is off, they go to On My iPhone → Downloads instead. Either way, the quickest way to find it is the download arrow in Safari's address bar: tap it, then tap the magnifying glass next to the file to jump straight to it in Files.

To change the folder, open Settings → Apps → Safari → Downloads (on iOS 17 and older it's Settings → Safari → Downloads). Choosing "On My iPhone" keeps big videos off your iCloud storage.

Things that trip people up on iPhone

The Paste button does nothing

If the little Paste bubble disappears before you tap it, nothing gets pasted. Long-press inside the link box and choose Paste from the menu instead, then tap Get Video.

I'm using Chrome on iPhone

Chrome on iOS can download files too, but it puts them in its own folder inside Files (On My iPhone → Chrome). Safari is simpler, and it's what this guide was written with.

The video plays but won't save to Photos

Check your storage in Settings → General → iPhone Storage. A full phone fails the Save Video step without a clear error. A 30-second clip at 1080p is usually 8 to 15 MB, so pick 720p if space is tight.

It says the pin has no video

The pin is probably a still image or a private pin. Our troubleshooting guide goes through each error message.

Frequently asked questions

Does this work on iPad?

Yes, the steps are identical. On iPad the Downloads folder is also in the Files app.

Will the video have a KlickPint watermark?

No. You get the file Pinterest stores, unchanged. If the creator put their own logo in the video, that stays.

Can I save Pinterest images the same way?

Yes. Paste an image pin's link and you'll get the original image file. Our image and GIF guide explains why that beats a screenshot.

Tip: Add KlickPint to your Home Screen. In Safari, tap Share → Add to Home Screen. It opens full screen like an app, so next time it's one tap away.
