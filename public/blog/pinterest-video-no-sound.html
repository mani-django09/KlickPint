Pinterest Video Has No Sound? Why It Happens and How to Fix It

Troubleshooting Published: November 2026 5 min read By Manikant Yadav

A silent Pinterest video is one of three things: it's muted, it never had sound in the first place, or something on your phone or computer is swallowing the audio. Two quick checks tell you which, and most of the time the fix takes a few seconds.

Check 1: Is it just muted?

Pinterest plays videos muted by default as you scroll the feed. That's the most common reason for "no sound", and it isn't a fault.

  • Tap the video to open the pin on its own.
  • Look for a small speaker icon on the video, usually in a corner, and tap it.
  • Turn your phone's volume up while the video is playing, so you're changing media volume rather than the ringer.

If sound comes on, you're done. If there's no speaker icon, or tapping it changes nothing, go to check 2.

Check 2: Did the video ever have sound?

Plenty of video pins are silent on purpose. Short loops, animated graphics, cinemagraphs and many GIF-style pins are uploaded without an audio track. Creators who expect people to watch muted in the feed often don't add any. When a pin has no audio, the speaker control is missing or does nothing, and there is no sound for any app or downloader to recover.

A quick way to tell: if the video has captions or text that tells the whole story, it was probably made to be watched silently.

Fixes when the pin has sound but you can't hear it

On a phone

  • Media volume. On Android, the volume buttons change the ringer unless media is playing. Start the video first, then press volume up, or open the volume panel and raise the media slider.
  • Bluetooth. If earbuds or a car are still connected, the sound goes there. Turn Bluetooth off for a moment and try again.
  • Do Not Disturb or Focus modes don't normally mute videos, but some battery-saver and "game" modes on Android phones limit media sound. Try with them off.
  • Update or reinstall the Pinterest app if sound works in other apps but not in Pinterest.

In a browser

  • Muted tab. In Chrome and Edge, right-click the Pinterest tab and choose Unmute site if it's there. Firefox shows a speaker icon on the tab itself.
  • Windows volume mixer. Right-click the speaker in the taskbar, open the volume mixer, and check that your browser isn't turned down to zero.
  • Site settings. If a browser is set to block sound for pinterest.com, allow it in the site settings (the icon to the left of the address).

Downloaded video has no sound

If the video plays with sound on Pinterest but your saved file is silent, work through these in order:

1

Did you save a GIF?

The GIF format can't hold sound at all. If you clicked a GIF button or saved the pin as a GIF, choose an MP4 quality instead (1080p, 720p and so on). Those files carry the audio track when the pin has one.

2

Is it a screen recording?

Screen recordings capture the screen, and on many phones the sound is off unless you turn on audio recording. A direct download is a better copy anyway.

3

Try another player

Pinterest's MP4 audio is a standard format that any modern player supports. If one app is silent, open the file in VLC (free on every platform). If it has sound there, the problem is the first app, not the file.

4

Check whether the file has an audio track

In VLC, open the file and go to Tools → Codec Information (on a Mac: Window → Media Information). If you only see a video stream and no audio stream, the source had no sound, and re-downloading won't change that.

Frequently asked questions

Can KlickPint add sound to a silent pin?

No. We give you the file Pinterest stores. If there is no audio in it, there's nothing to add back. Be wary of any tool that claims otherwise.

Why do some reposted videos have different music?

When a video moves between platforms, the person reposting sometimes swaps or removes the soundtrack. The original creator's version may sound different; our guide on finding a pin's creator helps you track it down.

Does the quality I pick change the sound?

The picture changes between 1080p, 720p, 480p and 360p. The audio is usually the same or very close across all of them, so pick the quality for the picture you need. See the quality guide.
