How to Download Pinterest Videos on Android

Tutorial Updated: October 2026 3 min read Works on Android 9+

Android makes this easier than iPhone does: Chrome's download manager and most Gallery apps talk to each other automatically, so there's no "move it to Photos" step at the end. Here's the full flow, plus where to find the file afterward.

The Fastest Method: KlickPint in Chrome

Any Android browser works, but Chrome is the default on most phones and has the smoothest download-notification flow. KlickPint (klickpint.online) is a website, not an app. There is nothing to install, nothing asking for storage or contacts permissions, no APK from a sketchy third-party store.

1

Copy the Pinterest Video Link

Open the Pinterest app, find the video, and tap the share icon (the arrow, usually bottom-right of the pin). Choose Copy Link. If you're on Pinterest's mobile website instead of the app, long-press the link and copy it the same way.

2

Open KlickPint

Switch to Chrome (or Samsung Internet, Firefox, whatever you use) and go to klickpint.online. Bookmark it or add it to your home screen: Chrome's menu has an "Add to Home screen" option that makes it launch like an app.

3

Paste and Pick a Quality

Tap Paste and KlickPint reads the link straight from your clipboard. Tap Get Video, and you'll see every quality Pinterest stored for that clip: usually 1080p, 720p, 480p, and 360p. Tap the one you want.

4

Check Your Notification Shade

Chrome downloads the file in the background and drops a notification when it's done. Swipe down to see it, or open Chrome's menu → Downloads. The file lands in your phone's shared Download folder, with no extra "save to gallery" tap needed for most launchers, since Android's media scanner picks up new videos there within a few seconds.

Where Exactly Does the File Go?

This trips people up more than any other step, because it varies slightly by phone. On stock Android and most Samsung/Pixel devices, downloaded videos land in Internal Storage/Download, and your Gallery or Google Photos app will show them in the main timeline within a minute because Android's MediaStore scanner indexes that folder automatically.

If it doesn't show up in Gallery after a minute or two, open the Files by Google app (or your phone's built-in "My Files" / "File Manager"), go to Downloads, and it'll be sitting there as an .mp4. You can open it directly or long-press to move/copy it into your Camera or Pictures folder if you want it in a specific album.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does this work on Samsung, Xiaomi, and OnePlus phones, or just Pixel?

Any Android phone with a modern browser works. KlickPint is a website, so it doesn't care about the manufacturer. Samsung Internet, MIUI's built-in browser, and Chrome all handle the download the same way.

My Gallery app isn't showing the downloaded video. What do I do?

Some Gallery apps (especially heavily customized ones on budget phones) only scan specific album folders, not the general Downloads folder. Open Files by Google → Downloads, find the video, and use "Move to" or "Copy to" to place it in your Camera or Pictures folder. It'll show up in Gallery immediately after that.

Do I need root access or a special app?

No. Root access is for modifying system-level Android permissions and has nothing to do with saving a video file, which any app with basic storage permission (which your browser already has) can do.

Can I download Pinterest videos on a Chromebook?

Yes. Chromebooks run the same Chrome browser and the flow is identical, except the file lands in your Chromebook's Downloads folder in the Files app rather than a phone Gallery.
