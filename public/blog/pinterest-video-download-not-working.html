Pinterest Video Won't Download? 7 Common Fixes

Troubleshooting Updated: October 2026 3 min read

Pasted a link and got an error, or nothing happened at all? It's almost always one of these seven things, and most take ten seconds to fix.

The 7 Most Common Causes

1

The pin is an image, not a video

Not every pin on Pinterest is a video. Plenty are static images or GIFs saved from other sites. If KlickPint says "no video found," open the pin on Pinterest itself and check whether it plays as a video with a progress bar. If it's just a still image, there's nothing to download.

2

The pin is private or on a secret board

KlickPint can only read publicly accessible pins. The same rule applies to every third-party tool, since private content requires being logged into the account that owns it. If the pin is on a secret board or the account is set to private, no downloader will be able to reach it.

3

You pasted a board or profile link instead of a pin link

Links like pinterest.com/username/board-name/ point to a whole board, not a single video. Open the specific pin you want, tap Share, and copy that pin's own link. It should contain /pin/ followed by a number.

4

The link got mangled during copy-paste

Some apps auto-add tracking parameters or accidentally include extra text when you copy a link. If paste isn't working, try manually selecting just the URL, or use Pinterest's Share → Copy Link option rather than copying from the address bar.

5

Pinterest is temporarily rate-limiting requests

Like any platform, Pinterest occasionally throttles automated-looking traffic for a few minutes at a time. If several pins in a row fail with a "service unavailable" message, that's what's happening, so wait 5 minutes and try again rather than retrying immediately, which can extend the cooldown.

6

Your browser is blocking the clipboard or the download

Some browsers (especially in Private/Incognito mode, or with strict privacy extensions) block clipboard access, which breaks the Paste button, so type or manually paste the link instead. If the download itself doesn't start, check whether your browser has file downloads disabled in its site settings for klickpint.online.

7

A VPN or firewall is interfering

Some corporate networks and VPN configurations block requests to video CDNs by default. If the video preview loads but the actual download hangs or fails, try switching off a VPN, or switching to mobile data, to see if the network is the cause.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why does it say "Could not extract Pin ID"?

This means the link you pasted doesn't contain a recognizable pin identifier, usually because it's a board, profile, or search-results link rather than a specific pin. Open the exact pin and copy its link again.

The preview loads but the download button doesn't work. Why?

This is almost always browser-related: an ad blocker or download-manager extension intercepting the request, or downloads disabled for the site in your browser's settings. Try a different browser to confirm, then check your extensions if the issue persists.

Does this happen more with certain types of pins?

Yes. Pins with very short lifespans (recently posted, still processing on Pinterest's end) sometimes don't have their video variants fully generated yet. Waiting a few minutes after a video is first posted usually resolves this.

Quick check: Before assuming something's broken, open the pin's link directly in your browser first. If Pinterest itself won't load the video, the problem is on Pinterest's end, not the downloader.
