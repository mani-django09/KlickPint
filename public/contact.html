Getting faster help with a download

The FAQ on the home page and the guide on what to do when a download is not working cover the usual causes. If you are still stuck, include:

  • the full Pinterest link you pasted (the pin must be public);
  • your device and browser, for example “iPhone, Safari”;
  • the exact message the tool showed, or a screenshot of it;
  • roughly when it happened, so we can find it in our logs.

What we cannot help with

We are not Pinterest, so we cannot restore accounts, remove pins from Pinterest or recover deleted content. To get a pin taken off Pinterest, report it to Pinterest directly. We also cannot save private pins or secret boards, because the tool only works with public ones.

Reporting an ad

Ads on this site are served by Google. If an ad looks misleading, inappropriate or like a fake download button, email legal@klickpint.online with a screenshot and the page it appeared on, and we will report it.
