What You Need to Submit a Valid DMCA Notice

Under 17 U.S.C. § 512(c)(3), a valid DMCA notice must include:

  • Your full legal name and contact information
  • Identification of the copyrighted work you claim has been infringed
  • The specific Pinterest URL(s) containing the allegedly infringing content
  • A statement that you have a good faith belief that the use is not authorized by the copyright owner
  • A statement that the information in the notice is accurate, under penalty of perjury
  • Your physical or electronic signature
Response time: We review all DMCA requests within 2–5 business days. Valid requests are actioned promptly.

Counter-Notification

If you believe content was removed in error, you may submit a counter-notification to dmca@klickpint.online with your full name, contact details, identification of the removed content, a statement under penalty of perjury that the removal was a mistake, and your consent to the jurisdiction of your local federal court.

False Claims Warning

Under Section 512(f) of the DMCA, any person who knowingly and materially misrepresents that content is infringing may be liable for damages, including costs and attorneys' fees. Submitting a false DMCA claim is a serious legal matter.
