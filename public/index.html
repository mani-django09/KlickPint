FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Straight answers to what people actually ask.

Is KlickPint really free? What's the catch?
There isn't one. No subscription, no card, no "basic vs premium" split. 1080p and unlimited downloads are free, and that isn't changing.
Does it work with pin.it short links?
Yes. Paste a full pinterest.com/pin/… URL or a short pin.it link from the app's Share button — both resolve automatically, no editing needed.
What quality options will I get?
Whatever Pinterest actually stored for that pin — usually 1080p, 720p, 480p and 360p. If a pin was uploaded at low resolution, 1080p simply won't exist for it. We show you the real list, not a fake one.
Do I need an account or a login?
No sign-up, no email, no verification code, no Pinterest login. Paste a URL, click Get Video, download. That's the whole process.
Does it work on iPhone and Android?
Yes — iPhone (Safari), Android (Chrome), iPad, Mac and Windows. If it has a browser, KlickPint works. On iOS the file saves to Files or Photos depending on your browser; the iPhone guide walks through it.
Will my video have a watermark?
Never from us. KlickPint hands you the original file from Pinterest's servers, untouched. If the creator burned a watermark into the video itself, that stays — we don't re-encode anything.
Can I download private pins?
No, by design. KlickPint only reads publicly accessible pins. Private pins and secret boards need a Pinterest login, and we never ask for one.
Is downloading Pinterest videos legal?
Downloading for personal, offline viewing is generally fine. Re-uploading someone else's video, or using it commercially without permission, is not. The copyright stays with whoever made it — please keep that in mind.

What actually happens when you paste a link

KlickPint's server asks Pinterest's own API for that pin's video data — the same data Pinterest uses to play the clip in your browser. We read the list of available qualities and show it to you. When you pick one, the file streams from Pinterest's CDN to your device. Nothing is written to our disk, and the link you pasted is discarded as soon as the response is sent.

Every Pinterest URL format works

  • Standard pin URLs — pinterest.com/pin/123456789/
  • Short links — pin.it/AbCdEf
  • Regional domains — pinterest.co.uk, pinterest.de, in.pinterest.com
  • Share links copied straight out of the mobile app
