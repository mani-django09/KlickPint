What actually happens when you use KlickPint

When you paste a Pinterest URL, KlickPint's server queries Pinterest's own API for that pin's video data — the same data Pinterest uses to play the video in your browser. We collect the available quality options (1080p, 720p, etc.) and present them to you. When you click a quality, we proxy the video file from Pinterest's CDN directly to your device. Nothing is stored on our end. The file goes straight from Pinterest's servers to your Downloads folder.
