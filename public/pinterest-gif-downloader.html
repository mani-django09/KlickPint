Turning a Pinterest MP4 into a GIF

If you got an MP4 but need a .gif, any free MP4-to-GIF converter will do it. Two settings decide whether the result is a sensible size:

  • Width around 480 pixels. GIF file size grows fast with dimensions. 480 is plenty for chats and forum posts.
  • About 15 frames per second. Most animations still look smooth, and the file is roughly half the size of a 30 fps GIF.

If you're comfortable with a terminal, ffmpeg does it in one line and gives cleaner colours than most websites:

ffmpeg -i pin.mp4 -vf "fps=15,scale=480:-1:flags=lanczos,split[a][b];[a]palettegen[p];[b][p]paletteuse" pin.gif

Why a GIF you saved isn't moving

Long-pressing an animated pin in the grid and choosing "Save image" often saves the still preview frame as a JPG, not the animation. Paste the pin's link here instead and you'll get the moving file. FAQ

Questions about Pinterest GIFs

How do I download a GIF from Pinterest?
Copy the pin's link, paste it above and tap Get GIF. If the pin is a real GIF, tap the GIF button to save the original animated file.
Why did I get an MP4 instead of a GIF?
Pinterest stores many animated pins as short silent videos rather than GIF files. When that's the case there is no GIF to download, so KlickPint gives you the MP4, which loops the same way and is usually much smaller.
Will the GIF keep its animation on iPhone?
Yes. Safari saves it to the Files app (Downloads). Open it, tap Share, then Save Image, and it shows up in Photos as an animated image. The Photos app plays it under the Animated album.
Can I send it on WhatsApp or Telegram?
Yes. Both accept GIFs and MP4s. Some chat apps convert GIFs to short videos when you send them, so don't be surprised if the other person sees it as a looping video.
Is there a size limit?
Not from us. You get the file Pinterest has, whatever its size. Very large GIFs can take a few seconds longer to download on mobile data.
Is downloading Pinterest GIFs allowed?
Saving one for personal use, like a reaction in a private chat, is generally fine. Reposting someone's animation as your own or using it in marketing needs their permission. Our copyright guide has the details.
